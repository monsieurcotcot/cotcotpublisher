Cotcot Publisher

A private tool that MonsieurCotCot, a streamer and video creator, uses to schedule and publish his own videos to his own social media accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that MonsieurCotCot made himself to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts.
  • Lets him choose the title, description, privacy setting and publication time of each video before it is sent.
  • Shows basic statistics of the videos it published (views, likes, comments).

Who can use it

Cotcot Publisher is a personal, self-hosted installation of the open-source scheduling software Postiz. It runs on a private server and is not open to the public: there is no sign-up, and the only accounts connected to it belong to its operator.
