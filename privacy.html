Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Cotcot Publisher handles, why, and how to have it deleted. Cotcot Publisher is a private tool operated by MonsieurCotCot.

1. Data the tool handles

When an account is connected, Cotcot Publisher receives from the platform, with the consent given on its authorisation screen:

  • Account profile: account identifier, display name, username and profile picture.
  • Access tokens: the tokens the platform issues so that the tool can publish on behalf of the account.
  • Videos and their details: the video files the user uploads to the tool, with their title, description, privacy setting and scheduled time.
  • Statistics: public figures of the account and of its videos, such as views, likes, comments and follower count.

For TikTok, this corresponds to the permissions user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload and video.publish. For YouTube, it corresponds to access to the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API for the connected channel.

2. What the data is used for

Only to do what the user asks: show which account is connected, upload and publish the user’s videos at the chosen time, and display the statistics of those videos. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. Videos and their details are sent only to the platform the user chose to publish on.

4. Storage and security

The data is stored on a private server operated by MonsieurCotCot, located in France. The tool is not reachable from the public internet: access requires a private, encrypted network and a personal login.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its tokens and profile data. Uploaded video files are deleted on request. A user can also revoke access at any time from the platform itself:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

To ask for the deletion of any data, write to monsieurcotcot.contact@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

6. Platform services

Cotcot Publisher uses the TikTok API and the YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. TikTok’s privacy policy is available at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies and no trackers. The tool itself uses a single session cookie, needed to stay logged in.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the bottom of this page shows the latest version.
